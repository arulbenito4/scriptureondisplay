Our Story

The right word at the right time

Scripture on Display began with a church calendar.

In 2026 I set out to design a wall calendar for our church family: a scenic photo for every month and a Bible verse for every day of the year. Choosing those verses became the best part. A verse for the first day of school. A verse for a long week. A verse for a season of waiting, and another for a season of harvest. Again and again we saw how the right word, at the right moment, speaks straight to the heart.

That is what Scripture on Display is about. God told His people to keep His words on their hearts and to make them part of everyday life:

"You shall write them on the doorposts of your house and on your gates."Deuteronomy 6:9

We make that simple. Our calendars, and soon our mugs, prints and apparel, put His Word in the places you look every day: on the kitchen wall, beside your desk, in your morning coffee.

Our mark is an S and a D drawn as one, with a sword running through it. It comes from Hebrews 4:12:

"The word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword … discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart."Hebrews 4:12

The words on your wall aren't decoration. They are alive, and they speak. Every product is designed verse by verse, with prayer, and we hope the verse in front of you today is exactly the one you need.

Arul

Founder, Scripture on Display
